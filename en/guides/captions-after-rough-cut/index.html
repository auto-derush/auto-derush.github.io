Captions: why to do them after the rough cut

2 min read

Captions have become essential, especially on social media. But doing them too early means you may have to start over.

The trap

If you generate captions on the raw clip and then cut silences, captions don't always follow the cuts: you have to realign or regenerate them.

The right order

  1. Rough cut: remove silences and bad takes.
  2. Edit: structure, choice of passages.
  3. Transcribe the edited sequence (Premiere Pro's Text panel).
  4. Captions created from that transcript.
  5. Style: size, position, color.

Why it's faster

  • The transcript is shorter: less text to review and fix.
  • Captions land in the right places straight away.
  • No caption line left over a removed silence.

Readability tips

  • 1 or 2 lines max, 35 to 42 characters per line.
  • Strong contrast (white text with outline or background).
  • On vertical video, place them in the middle, above app interfaces.
An automatic rough cut makes this method even more efficient: in seconds, the sequence is ready to be transcribed.
