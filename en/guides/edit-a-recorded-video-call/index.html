How to edit a webinar or recorded video call (Zoom, Teams…)

2 min read

Webinars, remote interviews, recorded meetings: these videos are full of gaps, lag and "can you hear me?". They clean up very well.

Before recording

In Zoom, enable the option to record a separate audio file for each participant. That's the key: every voice gets its own track, like one mic per person.

Import into Premiere

  • The call video on V1.
  • Each participant's audio on its own track (A1, A2…), synced with the video.

Remove the gaps

Calls are full of silence: lag, thinking time, waiting for screen share. Select the video and every audio track, then run Auto-dérush: silences are cut when nobody is talking, and every track stays aligned.

With several cameras

If you have each participant's video (a local recording, for example), Multicam edit (podcast) mode automatically shows whoever is talking, with a group shot when several people talk.

Finishing touches

  • Cut the technical moments ("can you hear me?", botched screen share).
  • Match participants' loudness in Essential Sound.
  • Add a title and the speakers' names.
A one-hour webinar often holds more than 10 minutes of gaps. Removing them automatically makes the replay far more watchable.
