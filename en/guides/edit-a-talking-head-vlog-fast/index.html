How to edit a talking-head vlog fast

2 min read

A talking-head vlog is often a single 20-minute take for 8 published minutes. Here's how to get there fast.

1. Shoot with the edit in mind

  • One continuous take, without stopping the camera between ideas.
  • If you flub a sentence, pause for 2 seconds and start over: the pause will be easy to spot.
  • Keep the mic as close to your mouth as possible (lavalier, or a boom just out of frame).

2. Remove silences in one click

Put the clip (and the external mic if you have one) on the timeline, select them, then run Auto-dérush with the Tight or Natural preset. You get a base with no dead air in seconds.

3. Pick the right takes

Play the sequence, keep the best version of each restarted sentence, ripple delete the others.

4. Dress up the cuts

  • Alternating zoom (110–120%) on every other cut.
  • B-roll and screenshots to illustrate.
  • Captions: generate them after the rough cut, on the edited sequence.

5. Sound and export

Normalize the voice (around -14 LUFS for YouTube), add subtle music, export.

Typical gain: on a 20-minute take, cutting silences alone often means more than half an hour by hand. Automated, it takes under a minute.
