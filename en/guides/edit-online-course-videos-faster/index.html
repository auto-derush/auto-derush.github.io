How to edit online course and tutorial videos faster

2 min read

An online course often means dozens of videos, all recorded with pauses to think, click or change slides. Cleaning them one by one takes days.

The problem with training videos

When you explain live, you pause: to find your words, open a menu, check a note. While recording, that's natural. On screen, those silences make students lose focus.

An efficient workflow

  1. Record each chapter in one take, without stopping at every hesitation: you'll cut afterwards.
  2. Import the screen recording (or camera) and the mic into Premiere, synced.
  3. Rough cut automatically: in Auto-dérush, the Loose preset keeps slightly longer pauses, giving viewers time to follow what happens on screen.
  4. Save your settings in My presets (for example "Course – screen recording") to reuse them on every video of the course.
  5. Touch up only the flubbed passages.

Watch out for useful silences

In a software tutorial, some silences show an action on screen: a menu opening, a render progressing. Two options:

  • raise the minimum silence length: only long pauses get cut;
  • or after the cut, click Undo cut if a particular video needs to keep its pace: the previous version is reopened.
Personal presets keep all your settings except the threshold, which is specific to each recording: remember to Analyze selection on each new clip.

The gain on a full course

Across 30 ten-minute videos, a manual rough cut can take several days. Automatically, each video is cleaned in under 30 seconds: you spend your time on content, not on cuts.
