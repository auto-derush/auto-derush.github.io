Freelance video editors: deliver faster without cutting corners

2 min read

As a freelancer, every hour spent on a repetitive task is an hour unbilled or underpaid. The rough cut is the first thing to automate.

Where the time goes

On an interview or podcast edit, a typical breakdown looks like this:

  • rough cut and silence removal: a big share of the time;
  • editorial choices and structure;
  • graphics, captions, sound;
  • export and back-and-forth with the client.

Only the first step is purely mechanical. That's the one to automate first.

Flat rate or hourly: you win either way

  • Flat rate: time saved directly raises your real hourly rate.
  • Hourly: you deliver faster, the client is happier, and you free up time for other projects.

A typical workflow

  1. Import, sync video and mic.
  2. Automatic rough cut with one preset per client or format.
  3. Editorial choices on an already clean base.
  4. Graphics and delivery.
