How much time does an automatic rough cut save?

2 min read

Cutting silences by hand feels quick one cut at a time. Added up, it's often the most time-consuming task in a speech-based edit.

Doing the math by hand

Take a 10-minute interview with about 150 silences to remove. For each silence, you have to:

  • find the start and end on the waveform;
  • make two cuts;
  • ripple delete;
  • listen to the transition again.

Even when fast, that's 8 to 15 seconds per silence: 20 to 40 minutes for 10 minutes of footage.

Doing the math automatically

With Auto-dérush: select the clips, one click, and the cut takes under 30 seconds for 10 minutes of footage. Add a few minutes to listen to the result: you stay under 5 minutes.

Over a month

  • YouTube creator, 2 videos a week from 15 minutes of footage each: roughly 4 to 8 hours saved per month.
  • Freelance editor, 5 interviews a week: easily 10 hours a month.
  • Weekly one-hour multicam video podcast: on top of silences, camera choices are made automatically, often several hours per episode.

What it's worth

At €5 a month, saving a few minutes is enough to pay for the tool. For an editor billing by the hour or by the project, time saved turns straight into margin, or into more videos.

The figures above are orders of magnitude: the real gain depends on how many pauses your footage has. The more hesitant the speech, the more you save.
