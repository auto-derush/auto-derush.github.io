How to improve interview audio in Premiere Pro

2 min read

Average picture gets a pass; average sound makes people leave. Here are the basic settings for clean interview audio, in the right order.

1. Pick the right source

If you have a lavalier and camera audio, use the lavalier as the main voice and mute or strongly lower the camera audio.

2. Clean up the noise

  • Enhance Speech (Essential Sound panel): very effective on noisy recordings. Go easy: at 100%, the voice can sound artificial.
  • Noise reduction in the audio effects, or in Audition for tough cases (hiss, air conditioning).

3. Even out the levels

  • Tag the clip as Dialogue in Essential Sound, then use Auto-Match loudness.
  • Aim for about -14 LUFS for YouTube, with peaks below -1 dB.

4. Then cut the silences

Clean the audio before the automatic rough cut: with lower background noise, the gap between voice and noise grows, and silence detection gets more accurate.

In Auto-dérush, click Analyze selection: you'll see the measured background noise and voice. If the gap is small, clean the audio and run the analysis again.

5. Finishing touches

Light compression makes the voice more even, and very quiet music under the voice masks small cuts.
