One rough-cut preset for each type of video

2 min read

A Short and a documentary aren't cut the same way. Having one preset per video type means consistent results without re-tweaking every time.

Auto-dérush's three base presets

  • Tight: silences cut from 300 ms, 50 ms padding before and 80 ms after. For Shorts, Reels and very fast-paced videos.
  • Natural: silences over 500 ms, 100 and 150 ms padding. For most YouTube videos.
  • Loose: silences over 800 ms, 200 and 300 ms padding. For courses, documentaries and laid-back podcasts.

A few recipes

  • Vertical Short: Tight, breaths cut.
  • Talking-head interview: Natural, breaths kept, "Cut other voices" if the interviewer is off camera.
  • Screen-recorded course: Loose, minimum silence length raised to 1,000 ms so viewers can see what happens on screen.
  • Multicam podcast: Natural, multicam mode, minimum shot length 2 s.

Saving your settings

In My presets, enter a name (for example "2-camera interview") and click Save. Every setting is kept, including track roles in multicam.

The volume threshold is never saved in a preset: it depends on each recording. Click Analyze selection on each new clip to get the right threshold.

Your presets are stored on your computer and survive plugin updates.
