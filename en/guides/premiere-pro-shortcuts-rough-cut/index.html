Premiere Pro keyboard shortcuts that speed up rough cuts

2 min read

Even with an automatic rough cut, some cuts are still done by hand. These shortcuts (Windows defaults) save a huge amount of time.

Playback and navigation

  • J, K, L: play backward, pause, play forward. Press L several times to speed up.
  • Up / Down: go to the previous or next edit point.
  • M: add a marker.

Cutting

  • Ctrl + K: Add Edit on targeted tracks at the playhead.
  • Ctrl + Shift + K: Add Edit on all tracks at the playhead.
  • C: Razor tool, to cut with a click.

Delete and close the gap

  • Shift + Delete: Ripple Delete (the gap closes).
  • Q: Ripple Trim Previous Edit to Playhead.
  • W: Ripple Trim Next Edit to Playhead.
Q and W are the fastest for a manual rough cut: place the playhead, one key, and it's cut and closed.

Selecting

  • I and O: In and Out points.
  • Ctrl + click on a track header: toggle track targeting.
