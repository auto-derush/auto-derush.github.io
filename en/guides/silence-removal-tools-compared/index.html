Removing silences in Premiere Pro: which tool should you choose?

2 min read

Several tools can save you from cutting silences by hand. They don't work the same way, and the right choice depends on your videos.

1. Premiere Pro's text-based editing

Recent versions of Premiere Pro transcribe the sequence and let you edit from the text. Pauses detected in the transcript can be deleted.

  • Strengths: built in, nothing to install; ideal if you already edit from text.
  • Limits: everything goes through the transcript (you have to transcribe first, in a supported language); little fine control over sound level, padding and breaths.

2. All-in-one extensions

Extensions like AutoCut bundle silence removal with other features: animated captions, automatic zooms, and so on.

  • Strengths: lots of features in one tool.
  • Limits: you also pay for features you may not use. Compare pricing and features against your needs.

3. Auto-dérush

Auto-dérush focuses on the rough cut: removing silences cleanly, across several tracks, without breaking audio sync.

  • Detection based on sound level, no transcription: works whatever language is spoken.
  • Every selected track cut together: video, lavalier and scratch audio stay aligned.
  • Suggested threshold based on background noise and voice, with a curve to check before cutting.
  • Breaths kept or cut, your choice.
  • Other voices: cuts the crew behind the camera by comparing mics.
  • Multicam podcasts: picks the camera of whoever is speaking.
  • Backup copy of the sequence before every cut.
  • €5 per month, cancel anytime.

Its limits, to be fair: no captions, it doesn't detect "um"s or flubbed sentences (that would need a transcript), and for now it runs on Windows only.

How to choose

  • You edit from text and your footage is simple: Premiere's text-based editing may be enough.
  • You want animated captions and zooms in the same tool: look at all-in-one extensions.
  • You want a reliable rough cut with an external mic, several voices or a multicam podcast, at a low price: that's what Auto-dérush is built for.
