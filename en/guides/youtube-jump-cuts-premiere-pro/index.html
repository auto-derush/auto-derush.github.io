How to make YouTube-style jump cuts in Premiere Pro

2 min read

The YouTuber talking-head style is a string of sentences with no dead air: jump cuts. Easy to understand, but very slow to do by hand.

What is a jump cut?

It's a cut within the same shot: the picture "jumps" slightly because a moment between two sentences was removed. On YouTube it has become a style of its own, adding energy and holding attention.

Making them fast

Most of the work in jump cuts is removing silences and hesitations. That's exactly what an automatic rough cut does:

  1. Select your talking-head clip.
  2. In Auto-dérush, choose Tight: silences cut from 300 ms, short padding.
  3. Click Cut silences.

In seconds you have the base of your jump cuts. What's left are the editorial cuts: flubbed sentences, duplicate takes.

Making the jumps look good

  • Alternating zoom: on every other cut, scale the picture to 110–120%. The jump becomes an intentional reframe.
  • B-roll: a screenshot or illustration over a cut hides the jump.
  • Leave some air: a pace that's too tight is tiring. The Natural preset keeps pauses under 500 ms.

Which preset for which format?

  • Shorts, Reels, TikTok: Tight, breaths cut.
  • Regular YouTube video: Natural.
  • Tutorial, course, podcast: Loose, to give viewers time to follow.
